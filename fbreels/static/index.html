Vidstash

Save any channel or page as MP4

Paste a YouTube or Facebook link, an @handle or a page name. Pick the videos you want — anything you already have is marked.

This is an online preview, so it can't download videos.

Vidstash does the downloading on a computer you control. Run it on your computer with ./app.sh and open http://127.0.0.1:8787: videos save straight into your folder. To use it from other devices, keep it running at home and follow “Use it from another device” in the README.

Keep it working
YouTube and Facebook change often. yt-dlp ships fixes within days — updating is the cure for most “it stopped working” problems. The app also updates yt-dlp every time you launch it with ./app.sh.
